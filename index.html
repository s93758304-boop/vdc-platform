Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a VDC account?

Create an account using your Nigerian phone number and verify it with the OTP sent to you.

Does VDC use email registration?

No. The VDC registration flow is designed around phone-number verification.

What are VIP levels?

VDC will provide up to 12 configurable VIP levels. Each level will display its price, benefits and applicable terms before activation.

How do withdrawals work?

Eligible withdrawals are handled in Nigerian Naira through supported Nigerian bank accounts during the withdrawal period configured by VDC.

Are displayed earnings guaranteed?

No. Any financial return or benefit displayed by VDC must be based on the actual applicable terms and underlying activity. The platform should not present example figures as guaranteed profits.
